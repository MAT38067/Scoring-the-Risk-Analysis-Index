The RAI-ICD has three different versions: each using a different set of ICD-10 codes to define cancer. Severe cancer (diagnoses with a 5-year survival rate of 33% or less), moderate cancer (diagnoses with a 5-year survival rate of 34%-66%), and mild cancer (diagnoses with a 5-year survival rate of 67% or greater) versions are all available in the RAI-ICD Scoring (3 cancer versions).do file. We recommend the use of the severe cancer version (also available in the RAI-ICD Scoring - R.R and RAI-ICD Scoring - SPSS.sps files), though we understand that you may want to select a different version for your specific purpose.
